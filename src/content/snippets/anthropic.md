Anthropic filed to go public on October 26. Nobody reads S-1s for fun, so here's the speedrun.

**Who they are:** an AI lab founded in 2021 by a group who left OpenAI, led by siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei. They make Claude. They talk about AI safety a lot, the way your one friend talks about CrossFit.

**How they make money:** mostly businesses and developers paying to use Claude through the API and in tools like Claude Code, plus subscriptions. More than 11,900 customers pay at least \$100,000 a year. About 250 pay more than \$10 million.

**The number that broke finance Twitter:** \$16.3 billion in revenue in the first half of 2026. For the entire year of 2025 it was \$4.6 billion. That's not a growth curve, that's a wall.

**The scary-looking number:** an \$80.7 billion net loss for the half. Relax. About \$72.4 billion of that is an accounting quirk from revaluing convertible notes as the company got more valuable. It's the corporate version of "I lost money because I got richer."

**The fine print most people missed:** a single special share held by the seven co-founders controls 50.1% of the shareholder vote. You can buy the stock. You cannot buy the steering wheel.

**The compute bill:** at least \$110 billion committed to Amazon's cloud through 2036, and up to \$120.5 billion with SpaceX through 2029. Yes, the rocket company.

Ticker: ANT, on Nasdaq. Underwriters: Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley. Last private valuation: \$965 billion.

The ant is small, but it can carry fifty times its weight.

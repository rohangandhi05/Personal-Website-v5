The name Blackstone is a hidden joke.

Its two founders were Stephen Schwarzman and Pete Peterson. "Schwarz" is German for black. "Peter" comes from the Greek word for stone. Black. Stone. Two finance guys in 1985 doing a riddle for their company name. Respect.

Today Blackstone is the biggest alternative asset manager on Earth, with a record \$1.35 trillion under management as of mid-2026. "Alternative" means anything that isn't plain stocks and bonds: private companies, real estate, private loans, infrastructure.

How it makes money:
1. **Management fees.** Pension funds, endowments and rich people hand over money, and Blackstone charges a yearly fee on it, usually a percent or two. On over a trillion dollars, that adds up.
2. **Performance fees.** When investments do well, Blackstone takes a share of the profits, often around 20%. This is the famous "2 and 20."

What it owns would surprise you. Blackstone is one of the biggest landlords in the world: warehouses, apartments, student housing, hotels, and, increasingly, the AI economy. In 2021 it bought QTS, a data center company, and its data center platform is now valued at around \$185 billion, with a pipeline of new builds behind it.

The newer push is getting regular wealthy individuals, not just institutions, to invest. Its private wealth business manages about \$300 billion.

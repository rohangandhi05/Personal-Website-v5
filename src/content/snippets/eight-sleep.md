October 20, 2025. Amazon Web Services has a massive outage, and a big chunk of the internet goes dark.

Most people lost access to some apps. Eight Sleep customers lost access to their beds.

Some \$2,000-plus smart beds got stuck upright. Some got stuck blasting heat in the middle of the night, because the bed couldn't reach the cloud to be told to stop. People literally lost sleep over a server problem. Eight Sleep apologized and rushed out an offline mode within days.

What Eight Sleep actually makes: the Pod, a cover that goes on top of your mattress with tiny water channels inside, connected to a hub that heats or cools the water. Each side of the bed can have its own temperature, so couples can finally stop fighting over the thermostat. It also tracks your sleep, heart rate and breathing, and adjusts temperature through the night automatically.

How it makes money:
1. **Hardware:** the Pod itself, which costs roughly \$2,000 to \$2,600 and up.
2. **A required membership:** "Autopilot," about \$15 to \$19 a month, which unlocks the automatic adjustments and sleep insights.

Hardware plus subscription is the dream combo: a big upfront sale, then a monthly payment forever. The company says it was free-cash-flow positive in 2025.

The latest funding twist: in March 2026 Eight Sleep raised \$50 million at a \$1.5 billion valuation, led by Tether, the crypto stablecoin company. It's also pursuing FDA clearance to detect sleep apnea.

Private. Founded in 2014 by CEO Matteo Franceschetti. Sleep tight. Stay connected.

**Experience:**

- *Your house (coming soon).* Laundry, dishes, tidying up. Still in early testing.

**Skills:** Helix (Figure's own AI model that lets me see, understand instructions and move), picking things up, not dropping them most of the time.

---

Behind the profile: Figure AI was founded in 2022 by Brett Adcock, who had already co-founded Archer Aviation, the air taxi company. Clearly the man only starts companies that look like movie props.

How it makes money: barely, so far. The plan is to lease robots to factories and warehouses as a monthly service, like hiring a worker through an agency, and eventually sell them to homes. Humanoid robots are expensive to build and still early in actually doing useful work at scale.

Investors don't care yet. In September 2025 Figure raised money at a \$39 billion valuation, making it the most valuable humanoid robot startup in the world. Skeptics point out the valuation is running well ahead of the robots actually deployed.

It also had a partnership with OpenAI, then ended it in 2025 to build its own AI in-house.

Private. Open to work. Will relocate.

**PATIENT REPORT: Function Health**

**Test ordered:** Basically all of them.

At a normal annual checkup, your doctor might order a handful of blood tests. Function Health runs over 100 at once, now more than 160 across the year: hormones, heart health, inflammation, nutrients, thyroid, early disease markers, plus things you didn't know could be tested. Then it puts the results in an app with explanations and recommendations.

**How it makes money:** a membership. It started at about \$499 a year for the core panel, with add-ons for more advanced tests. You go to a lab like Quest, they draw your blood, and Function handles the rest.

**Founders:** entrepreneur Jonathan Swerdlin and Dr. Mark Hyman, a well-known functional medicine doctor and bestselling author.

**Notable findings:**
- Function is riding the longevity wave: people who want to catch problems early and optimize everything, not just treat illness.
- In April 2026 it bought **Ezra**, a company that does full-body MRI scans, so members can get imaging on top of bloodwork. It also picked up Getlabs, for at-home blood draws, and SuppCo, a supplement startup.
- It raised a \$298 million Series B at a \$2.5 billion valuation in November 2025, plus \$450 million in growth financing from General Catalyst in July 2026.

**Second opinion:** many doctors caution that running hundreds of tests on healthy people can lead to false alarms, anxiety and unnecessary follow-ups. Function argues knowing more is better than knowing less.

**Diagnosis:** Private. Prognosis: you will now refresh your cholesterol numbers like they're stock prices.

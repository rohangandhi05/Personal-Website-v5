Google's character sheet, RPG style.

**Main quest: ads.** You search for "best running shoes," a company pays for the top result. In 2025 Google Services, mostly ads across Search, YouTube and other sites, made up about 85% of Alphabet's revenue. In the April–June 2026 quarter, Search ads alone brought in \$63.3 billion.

**Side quest 1: Google Cloud.** Renting out computing power and AI to other companies. This used to be the side quest everyone skipped. In that same quarter it grew 82% to \$24.8 billion, and Alphabet has \$514 billion in signed cloud contracts waiting to be delivered.

**Secret item: TPUs.** Google has been designing its own AI chips since 2015, years before the AI boom. It's one of the few companies that doesn't have to wait in line for Nvidia.

**Side quest 2: YouTube.** About \$11 billion in ad revenue that quarter, plus Premium and YouTube TV subscriptions.

**Side quest 3: "Other Bets."** Waymo's self-driving taxis, life sciences and other moonshots. Combined, they lost \$1.8 billion that quarter. Moonshots are expensive.

**The weird item in the inventory:** Google pays Apple billions a year to be the default search engine on iPhones. The antitrust trial put the 2022 number at about \$20 billion.

**Upcoming boss fight:** capital spending of \$175 to \$185 billion this year, mostly data centers, roughly double 2025.

The parent company is called Alphabet because founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin wanted a holding company for all the side quests. Also, alpha-bet.

Public (NASDAQ: GOOGL). Level 99 in ads. Still grinding.

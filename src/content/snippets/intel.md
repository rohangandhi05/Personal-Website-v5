*Previously, on Intel...*

**Season 1 (1968).** Gordon Moore and Robert Noyce found Intel. Moore predicts the number of transistors on a chip will keep doubling. The industry spends the next 50 years making him right.

**Season 2 (1990s).** "Intel Inside." Five-note jingle. Every PC on Earth. Intel is the king.

**Season 3 (2007).** Apple asks Intel to make the chip for a new phone. Intel passes because it doesn't think the price works. The phone is the iPhone. Later CEO Paul Otellini admits he got it wrong.

**Season 4 (2010s–2024).** Manufacturing delays. TSMC pulls ahead. Apple leaves to make its own chips. AMD comes back from the dead. Nvidia wins AI. Intel is no longer the king.

**Season 5 (2025): the plot twist.** New CEO Lip-Bu Tan. The U.S. government takes a 9.9% stake in Intel, turning grants into shares. SoftBank invests \$2 billion. Nvidia invests \$5 billion. Basically everyone decided the U.S. can't afford for its only big American chipmaker to fail.

How it makes money: selling processors for PCs and servers, which is still most of its revenue. The expensive bet is Intel Foundry, making chips for other companies in U.S. factories, the way TSMC does in Taiwan. It's a national security story dressed up as a business plan.

Public (NASDAQ: INTC). Partly owned by your tax dollars, which technically makes you a very small shareholder.

*Next season: can it win back the throne?*

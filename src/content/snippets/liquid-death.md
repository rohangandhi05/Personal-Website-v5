Liquid Death sells water.

That's it. Water. In a tallboy can that looks like it should be confiscated at a high school party. Slogan: "Murder your thirst."

And somehow that's a company that reportedly did around \$333 million in revenue in 2024 and was valued at \$1.4 billion that year.

How: the founder, Mike Cessario, is a former Netflix creative director, and it shows. Liquid Death is a marketing company that happens to have a water supply. The water comes from the Alps. The brand comes from a heavy metal album cover.

A partial list of things this water company has actually done:

- Made fans sign a contract handing over their soul to join its "Country Club"
- Sold skateboards painted with Tony Hawk's actual blood
- Launched iced teas with names like Armless Palmer

How it makes money is refreshingly normal: it sells cans through grocery stores, gas stations and big chains, plus merch. The pitch is that it's the thing you can hold at a bar or festival when you're not drinking but don't want to look like you're not drinking.

It's also the cleanest example of a lesson the beverage aisle keeps relearning: people don't buy the water. They buy who they get to be while holding it.

Private. Backed by investors including Science Inc, plus celebrities like Josh Brolin. Thanos owns a piece of the water company. Of course he does.

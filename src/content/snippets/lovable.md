Sweden's greatest exports, ranked:

1. IKEA
2. ABBA
3. Spotify
4. Lovable (rising fast)

Lovable is a Stockholm startup where you describe an app or website in plain English and its AI builds it for you, front to back. It's like IKEA, except the furniture assembles itself and you don't end up with three mystery screws.

It started as an open-source side project. In 2023 founder Anton Osika released GPT Engineer, a tool that let AI write entire codebases. It went viral on GitHub. Osika turned it into a company, renamed it Lovable, and aimed it at people who've never written code: founders, designers, marketers, small business owners.

How it makes money: subscriptions with credits. You pay monthly, and each request to the AI uses up some credits. Bigger projects, more credits, higher plans. Companies are now buying it for teams too.

The growth is absurd. It crossed \$400 million in annual recurring revenue in February 2026 and passed \$600 million by late September. Early on it did this with a tiny team, reportedly over \$2.7 million in revenue per employee at one point. Most software companies are happy with a tenth of that.

In August 2026 it raised \$400 million at a \$13.3 billion valuation, led by Menlo Ventures, double what it was worth eight months earlier. It's now one of the most valuable startups in Europe.

Private. Tack så mycket, Sweden.

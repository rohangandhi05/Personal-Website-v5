Meta is a family with two kids.

The older kid has a real job. That's ads. Every time you scroll Instagram, Facebook or Threads, advertisers pay to put something in front of you, and Meta is frighteningly good at picking what. In the last quarter of 2025 that kid brought home \$58.1 billion.

The younger kid wants to be an artist. That's Reality Labs: VR headsets, the metaverse, and the Ray-Ban smart glasses made with EssilorLuxottica. In 2025 it lost \$19.1 billion on about \$2.2 billion of revenue. Since late 2020, its total operating losses have passed \$80 billion.

That is the most expensive gap year in history.

What most people don't know: WhatsApp, which has billions of users and no ads in your chats, makes money from businesses. Airlines, banks and stores pay Meta to message you there. In much of the world WhatsApp is how you talk to companies, and that line of revenue is growing fast.

Zuckerberg says 2026 will likely be the peak year for Reality Labs losses, and the money is shifting from VR headsets to glasses, which he calls "some of the fastest-growing consumer electronics in history."

Meanwhile the older kid is also paying for an enormous AI data center buildout, and Meta keeps raising how much it plans to spend.

Public (NASDAQ: META). The company renamed itself after the younger kid. The older kid still pays the rent.

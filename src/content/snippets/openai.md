OpenAI, a timeline:

**2015.** A nonprofit research lab. Founders include Sam Altman and Elon Musk. Mission: make sure AI benefits humanity. Business model: none, on purpose.

**2019.** Turns out training AI costs a fortune. OpenAI creates a "capped-profit" arm so it can raise real money. Microsoft writes a \$1 billion check.

**2022.** ChatGPT launches as a "research preview." It becomes the fastest-growing consumer app anyone had seen. The research preview is now the entire company.

**2025.** Restructures into a public benefit corporation. Microsoft walks away holding a stake worth more than most countries' stock markets.

**March 2026.** Raises \$122 billion, the biggest private round in Silicon Valley history, at an \$852 billion valuation. Amazon, Nvidia and SoftBank anchor it. Revenue is running at about \$2 billion a month.

**April 2026.** Buys TBPN. Yes, that TBPN. The show this whole list of companies came from. The nonprofit now owns a three-hour daily live talk show with a gong.

How it makes money: ChatGPT subscriptions (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise), plus developers paying per token to use its models through the API. How it spends money: on compute, in amounts that make the revenue look like a tip jar. It still isn't profitable.

Private, for now. Reports say an IPO could come in 2027.

From "no business model, on purpose" to the most valuable startup ever. Character development.

Rivian makes electric trucks and SUVs that look like a golden retriever would design a car: friendly face, oval headlights, extremely outdoorsy.

That's the part you know. Here's the part you don't.

**Amazon is its biggest customer.** Amazon invested early and ordered 100,000 electric delivery vans. If you've seen an Amazon van with a weird smiling face, that's a Rivian.

**Volkswagen is paying it for software.** In 2024 Volkswagen, one of the biggest carmakers on Earth, formed a joint venture with Rivian to use Rivian's electrical architecture and software in VW cars, committing up to several billion dollars. In the April–June 2026 quarter, about 60% of Rivian's software and services revenue came from that VW venture. A 17-year-old startup is selling the brains of the car to an 89-year-old giant.

How it makes money overall: mostly selling vehicles (R1T truck, R1S SUV, commercial vans), plus software, services and regulatory credits it can sell to other automakers.

The big 2026 moment: the R2. A smaller, cheaper SUV meant to bring Rivian to normal people, not just people with \$80,000 and a kayak. Deliveries started in June with a \$58,000 launch edition, and cheaper trims are planned for early 2027. Q2 revenue rose 27% to \$1.66 billion.

Founder RJ Scaringe started the company in 2009, years before EVs were cool, and spent a decade in near-stealth.

Public (NASDAQ: RIVN). Adventure-ready, and now selling the map to Volkswagen.

Bret Taylor's résumé, abridged:

- Co-created **Google Maps**
- Co-founded **FriendFeed**, which Facebook bought
- **Chief technology officer of Facebook**
- Co-CEO of **Salesforce**
- Chairman of **Twitter's** board during Elon Musk's takeover
- Chairman of **OpenAI's** board after Sam Altman's firing and return

And then, in 2023, he co-founded Sierra with Clay Bavor, a longtime Google executive.

What Sierra does: AI agents that handle customer service for big brands. When you chat with or call a company about a return, a billing question or a cancellation, Sierra's AI can handle the whole conversation, take actions like processing the refund, and talk like a polished human rep. Customers include SiriusXM, ADT, Cigna and Rivian.

How it makes money, and this is the clever bit: **outcome-based pricing.** Sierra mostly charges when the AI actually solves the customer's problem. If the AI can't handle it and has to transfer you to a real person, the company often doesn't pay for that conversation. Instead of charging per seat or per login, it charges per success. It's like a lawyer working on contingency, but for "where's my package."

It's growing fast. In May 2026 Sierra raised \$950 million at about a \$15.8 billion valuation. By late October it said it had passed \$400 million in annual recurring revenue, adding more than \$150 million in just the third quarter.

Private. The help desk has a new hire, and it has the best résumé in Silicon Valley.

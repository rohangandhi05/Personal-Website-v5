Grand Theft Auto VI came out on November 19, 2026. It took 13 years.

GTA V came out in 2013. Since then a kid could be born, start middle school, and develop opinions about Vice City, all while waiting for the sequel. Rockstar's first trailer for GTA VI in December 2023 broke YouTube viewing records. Then came delays, more trailers, and an entire internet genre of "GTA 6 before ___" jokes.

GTA 6 before... nothing anymore. It's out.

And the numbers: Sensor Tower estimates the game made about \$1.84 billion by release day, more than double GTA V's roughly \$800 million first day. These are estimates, since Take-Two hasn't released official numbers yet.

The company behind it is Take-Two Interactive, and most people have no idea it exists. It owns:

- **Rockstar Games:** GTA and Red Dead Redemption
- **2K:** NBA 2K, Borderlands, BioShock, Civilization
- **Zynga:** FarmVille and Words With Friends, bought for \$12.7 billion in 2022

How it makes money: selling games, and then selling things inside games. GTA Online has turned GTA V into a money machine for over a decade through in-game currency "Shark Cards." GTA V has sold well over 200 million copies.

CEO Strauss Zelnick (a recent TBPN guest) told investors the launch should drive record results this fiscal year, guiding to \$8 billion or more in bookings.

Public (NASDAQ: TTWO). Wasted? Never. This was the most expensive wait in entertainment history, and it paid off.

Does AI need one giant genius model for everything? Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman: "You don't need a Michelin chef to make you breakfast. It's okay to have some eggs on toast."

Translation: let small, cheap AI handle the easy stuff and save the big brain for the hard questions. Nobody needs a PhD to tell them what time it is.

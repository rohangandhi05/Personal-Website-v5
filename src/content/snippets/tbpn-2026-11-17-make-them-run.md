Rule one of fighting Goliath: don't shop at Goliath's grocery store.

Andrew Feldman, co-founder and CEO of Cerebras, showed up to TBPN at TomorrowX with the chip in hand. "This is the largest chip in the history of the computer industry. The fastest AI chip."

The better part was how you take on Nvidia without getting flattened by Nvidia's size. "One of the things you do when you're David going up against Goliath is you think really hard about how to not be in places where their size matters."

So Cerebras built a supply chain that barely touches Nvidia's. At TSMC it makes chips on the 5 nanometer node, not the 3 nanometer one where Nvidia hoovers up capacity.

It also skips HBM, the pricey high-bandwidth memory, because Nvidia is "one of the largest buyers of memory on Earth." Trying to outbid them there is like trying to outbid Costco on hot dogs.

His whole philosophy fits in five words: "If they're big, make them run." He said it, fittingly, inside a basketball arena.

The Mighty Ducks playbook, but with lithography.

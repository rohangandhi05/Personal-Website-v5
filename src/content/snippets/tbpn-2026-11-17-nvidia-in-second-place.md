Everyone knows Nvidia. Fewer people know Cerebras, which makes an AI chip the size of a dinner plate and, per independent testers, runs AI more than twice as fast as the next-fastest provider. CEO Andrew Feldman's pitch on TBPN: "The fastest AI chip."

Nvidia still sells way more chips. But on speed? As Ricky Bobby said, if you ain't first, you're last.

"There's no coincidences in this world."

That's how TBPN opened its investigation into why Anthropic went public on the same day GTA VI launched. What followed was the finest red-string corkboard work since Charlie's Pepe Silvia wall in It's Always Sunny.

Exhibit A: Claude Shannon, the father of information theory and Claude's namesake, died in 2001. That same year, Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto III. The protagonist's name? Claude.

Exhibit B: GTA VI was originally set for May 26, 2026. Per the show, Anthropic released version 3.3 of its responsible scaling policy on May 26.

Exhibit C: GTA VI then slipped 177 days, to November 19. Anthropic's valuation at its IPO, per the show: \$1.77 trillion.

Exhibit D: both companies were founded by siblings. Sam and Dan Houser at Rockstar. Dario and Daniela Amodei at Anthropic.

Exhibit E: Anthropic priced its IPO at \$83 a share. Dario Amodei was born in 1983.

Even the set had to step in on that one: that's a link "between two Anthropic things. Not even Anthropic and GTA."

The response: "You're telling me we're seeing patterns that don't exist?"

Yes. But respectfully, so is the stock market.
